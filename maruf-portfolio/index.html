How long does a project take?

A landing page usually takes 3–7 days. A full website or app takes 2–4 weeks, depending on the number of screens.

What do I need to get started?

Just your idea, your goals and any brand materials you have. If you don't have a brand yet, I can create one.

Do you also build the website?

I focus on design, and I deliver clean Figma files ready for developers. I can also build on Webflow or Framer when needed.

How many revisions do I get?

Every project includes revision rounds, so you are happy with the final result.

How do payments work?

You can hire me through Fiverr or directly. Direct projects usually start with a 50% deposit.
